Dikshant Sharma

I am a Research Assistant at the Vision, Language and Learning Group, IIT Jodhpur, advised by Dr. Anand Mishra. My research focuses on multimodal learning, particularly scene-text-aware and cross-lingual image–text retrieval. I am interested in developing retrieval systems that can jointly understand visual content, natural-language descriptions, and text appearing within images across multilingual settings.

I am also working on vision-language models for autonomous driving through a Bosch research project, in collaboration with Yasaswi Bharadwaj Katta , Research Scientist at Bosch, and Dr. Sahana Prabhu from IIIT Bangalore. This work explores the use of vision-language models for understanding road scenes and supporting reliable visual reasoning in autonomous-driving environments.

I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science with a specialization in AI and Machine Learning from the Central University of Rajasthan, and my B.C.A. from the University of Rajasthan. I qualified UGC-NET JRF in Computer Science and Applications (Top 0.6%) and GATE in Computer Science and Engineering.
